# Realistic 90-day visibility and client acquisition plan

**Starting point:** Zero guaranteed site traffic, zero assumed paying clients, and no paid ads budget. Use unpaid channels and real work before considering spend. This is a task plan, not a traffic or revenue prediction.

## Days 1–15 — Get the foundation live

- Publish GitHub Pages to `shamim98.com` with HTTPS; verify all five indexable URLs, social preview, robots and sitemap.
- Create and verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools properties. Submit sitemap. Set up GA4 only with a real ID and reviewed consent.
- Open the site on actual phones/tablets and test that the main CTA is visible, the menu closes and the mailto draft works. Measure baseline Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals via PageSpeed Insights after launch.
- Verify the GitHub profile, LinkedIn profile and existing Google Scholar link. Do not fabricate new business social handles.
- Prepare three easy-to-understand offers: (a) one-page landing site, (b) small business site, (c) later-stage ecommerce/Laravel. Do not promise fixed prices or deadlines without scope.

**Evidence to collect:** indexed pages, screenshot proof, Search Console baseline, form/contact behavior, one-line service descriptions.

## Days 16–30 — Turn work into proof

- Complete a transparent Double Generation case study (your role, the real tech you used, screenshots with permission, in-development status).
- Add one small-business landing-page design walkthrough using Glow Studio or Gather & Grain, explicitly identified as a concept.
- Publish one helpful article about what businesses should prepare before commissioning a website (text, images, goals, access, legal disclosures).
- Create founder-led LinkedIn posts: intro + portfolio tour + design decision + one research-informed engineering lesson. Focus on being useful, not posting filler.
- Start a small, manual, permission-conscious outreach test to relevant small business operators. Personalize the note, never imply a prior relationship, respect platform anti-spam rules, and do not automate unsolicited bulk messages.

**Evidence to collect:** actual replies, common objections, interests, what portfolio links people click, whether visitors understand the service.

## Days 31–60 — Improve targeted discovery

- Review Search Console queries and indexation; fix broken titles/links and add links to the most relevant service pages.
- Publish a practical one-page vs. multi-page decision article and a focused technical article on LMS or Laravel workflows.
- Add one technically grounded n8n workflow explainer clearly labeling what is prototype vs. deployed.
- Maintain a sustainable routine: roughly one helpful article or case-study update every two weeks and two credible professional social posts weekly, adjusting for available time.
- Review contact clicks, draft attempts and actual inbound inquiries. Remember that a button click is **not** necessarily a paying lead.
- Offer to prepare bespoke, permitted-scope website concepts for promising leads after discussion; never copy customer assets without consent.

**Evidence to collect:** qualified discussions, which pages bring inquiries, search impressions, problem statements, time required for a typical build.

## Days 61–90 — Convert learning into a repeatable offer

- Revise service-page copy using actual questions from prospects: scope, revisions, hosting, project ownership, handover and maintenance.
- If you have consent and evidence, publish a finished small project case study; otherwise improve honest concept walkthroughs.
- Build a simple pre-project questionnaire or a verified online inquiry provider only if needed; maintain the mailto fallback until submission is tested.
- Review page speed and accessibility on the live domain and fix the largest observed bottlenecks first.
- Decide whether specialized research, PHP/Laravel, ecommerce or n8n services deserve additional pages based on demand and actual delivered work, not keyword volume alone.
- If ready for Meta ads, complete business verification, audience/consent/legal checks, pixel verification and a small controlled test budget — do not introduce tracking by default.

**Evidence to collect:** total *qualified* inquiries, proposals sent, projects agreed, delivery hours, source of contact, organic impressions/clicks, successful handovers. Do not treat unsupported session metrics as sales.

## Low-cost channel strategy

| Channel | Practical organic action | What to learn |
|---|---|---|
| Google / Bing | 4 substantive service pages, 1 honest case study, 2 useful guides | Search intent, indexing, relevant inquiries |
| LinkedIn | Founder profile with accurate academic publications + project screenshots | Business questions and professional referrals |
| Facebook / Instagram | Short screen-recordings of motion websites, with clear concept labels | Which niches respond to visual examples |
| GitHub | Public code demos with safe configuration and no private client data | Trust in coding quality and scope clarity |
| Referrals | Ask completed clients (if any) whether they would introduce someone | Real value and delivery experience |

**No promised ranking, traffic volume or client count.** International visibility takes repetition, proof and feedback. Check any new claim against source evidence before publishing it.
