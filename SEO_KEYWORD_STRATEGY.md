Competitive pressure labels below are editorial estimates based on those SERP patterns and the general breadth of each term, **not measured keyword-difficulty scores**. Re-evaluate with actual Google Search Console impressions, searches and competitors after launch.

## Content architecture

- Homepage remains the umbrella introduction and author credibility hub, with all eight services.
- Initial substantive pages: landing page design, small business websites, n8n/AI automation, PHP/Laravel/LMS.
- `Privacy` is useful for visitors but intentionally excluded from XML sitemap and marked `noindex,follow`.
- Do **not** generate separate geo-pages for the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, or Bangladesh using essentially identical copy. Add market-specific pages only when you have genuine distinct information or relevant work.
- Do **not** build fictional reviews, schema ratings, case-study ROI, fabricated certifications or “guaranteed Google ranking” language.

## Content priorities after launch

1. **Double Generation case study:** once verified, write what you actually built (scope, stack, your role, screens with permission, current limitations, lessons). Explain that the site is under construction.
2. **Landing-page brief guide:** "What a first small-business website needs before design starts" — target prospective clients, not other developers. Include a useful brief checklist.
3. **One-page versus multi-page:** practical decision guide based on catalog size, updates, trust pages and contact needs.
4. **LMS development notes:** a transparent development diary or architecture overview without exposing private code or claiming unimplemented features.
5. **n8n workflow architecture:** an illustrated, honest explanation of human-reviewed business discovery or inquiry sorting; do not publish private lead lists.
6. **Research-to-development perspective:** show how testing, validation and reproducibility influence your software process. Publications support credibility but do not imply universities endorse the agency.

## Technical guardrails

- Each indexable page has one meaningful H1, a distinct title, distinct description, unique canonical URL and internal links.
- Robots.txt and sitemap.xml contain only intended canonical paths.
- Structured data describes real people, pages and services; none includes made-up ratings, reviews or a fictional office address.
- Avoid adding 50 service/city pages without evidence of genuinely distinct value.
- Recheck live Search Console impressions, click-through rates and actual lead quality before changing page targets.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Google's Search Central documentation emphasizes useful, people-first content, canonical consistency and genuine site structure rather than automatic ranking tricks. Current SERP examples contain significant directories and established freelance providers. Relative pressure estimates here are **not** validated search-volume forecasts. Paid keyword data and regional SERP comparisons are still needed for numerical priority scoring.
