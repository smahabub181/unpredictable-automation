How this portfolio handles inquiry information and optional measurement. Last revised 18 November 2026.

What this website does by default

This portfolio is published as static website files. It does not have an account system, checkout or server-side contact database. The project brief form opens an email draft in your own email application unless the founder has activated a separate form provider.

If you contact me via email or WhatsApp, that provider handles your message according to its own terms and privacy policy. Please avoid sending passwords, financial credentials, medical data or sensitive research materials through an initial inquiry.

Optional analytics and advertising technology

The website contains optional configuration for Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel. Neither is active unless genuine configuration IDs have been added and you grant the respective consent. You can change your choice using the Cookie preferences link. Only the consent choice itself is stored in your browser's local storage, if available.

When enabled and consented to, those providers may receive usage data and set cookies under their terms. Essential fonts may be requested from Google Fonts. Refer to the providers' own policies for their processing practices. No analytics or advertising results are claimed before real configuration and traffic exist.

Contact form and data handling

Without a configured form provider, the website does not receive or store submitted form contents. An email draft must be sent from your email application. If a third-party form provider is subsequently enabled, that service will receive your name, project description and any information you choose to include; the live disclosure must then be updated with provider name, retention period and applicable rights before activation.

Your choices

You may decline non-essential tracking and continue using the website. To request access to, correction of or deletion of information you sent directly by email, please write to shamim.mahbub.hasan@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed according to applicable law and the nature of the information involved.

This notice describes the current static-site implementation, not a legal certification for every country where the website can be visited. Review it with qualified advice before adding advertising campaigns or a form processor.
