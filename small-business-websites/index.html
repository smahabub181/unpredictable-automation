Who this is for

Businesses with only a social-media presence often need one reliable destination for essential details, while established stores may need a tailored catalog or ecommerce experience. The right architecture depends on products, content, fulfillment and maintenance needs.

A sensible way to start

We define the pages and content first, then prioritize responsive design and a working route to inquiry or purchase. If your project needs inventory, payment processing or a custom back office, those are separately scoped instead of presented as features that every site automatically includes.

Common questions

Do I need a full online shop immediately?

Not always. A small website with a catalog and direct inquiry method may be enough to start, depending on your business.

Can you build a custom backend?

I work with PHP and Laravel. For stores requiring accounts, payments, orders or inventory, I would define the data and operational requirements before estimating delivery.
